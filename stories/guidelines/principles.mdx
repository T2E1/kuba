# Principles

Principles exist to guide decisions and take the subjectivity out of them.
Each one can be captured in a single word or a short phrase, always paired
with a clear explanation of why it exists.

You can keep principles broad, like the three below, or split them into
sub-groups — principles that guide the team's attitude, principles for
product experience, principles specific to your Design System. Feel free
to draw whatever division makes sense for your context; just make sure
they stay clear and tangible.

## Simple

We aim to be simple in how each component is built, in the rules we write,
and in how we apply Kuba to build any digital product. An element that
needs extensive documentation to be understood is probably doing too much.

Keywords: easy; direct; clear; focused.

## Accessible

We design for everyone — which is why we never skip accessibility
criteria in any design or code decision. Every visual component in this
Storybook ships an Accessibility panel (`addon-a11y`), so a violation is
visible at the point of use, not discovered later in production.

Keywords: inclusive; considerate; universal.

## Flexible

Components must adapt to the logic of multiple brands and different
scenarios — without forking the component for each variation. That's what
Design Tokens and themes make possible: the same `<kb-button>`, with
different looks, just by swapping which tokens are active.

Keywords: adaptable; versatile; dynamic.
