# Use & collaborate

Kuba is at version `0.1.0-alpha` and under active development. Use it,
give lots of feedback, and contribute to that evolution. The
**Guidelines** section explains how you can use Kuba to design and build
your products, or even craft your own architecture on top of it.

Kuba is distributed under the **MIT** license — you can use it for any
purpose, commercial included; just keep the credits in the
[`LICENSE`](https://github.com/T2E1/kuba/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Send your feedback

Found something that could be better? Don't hold back the suggestion —
open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/T2E1/kuba/issues). Positive
feedback is welcome too: knowing what's working helps as much as knowing
what isn't.

## GitHub

The repository is open at
[github.com/T2E1/kuba](https://github.com/T2E1/kuba), with the `README`
covering the main instructions. There you'll find the Design Tokens that
power Kuba's demo brand and every component listed in this Storybook.

## Contribute a change

Every contribution follows [Conventional
Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/) — enforced by `commitlint`
on each commit — and passes `npm run check` (lint and formatting via
Biome) before being accepted. A new component needs, at minimum, a
documented `types.d.ts` and, if visual, a story — see **Guidelines/Naming**
for where it fits (Element or Block).

## Duplicate and use freely

Starting your own product and want to use Kuba as its foundation? Go ahead.
Install via `npm install @t2e1/kuba`, or clone the repository and use the
package structure as a starting point — see **Guidelines/Try Kuba**. Adapt
the Design Tokens to your brand's variables and adjust the components as
needed.

**Important**: in that case, just remember to keep the license credits, as
required by the MIT license.
