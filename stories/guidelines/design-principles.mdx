# Design principles

It all comes down to visual simplicity. Every `Design Token` exists to
remove an arbitrary decision — not to multiply options. The spacing
scale, the per-brand color palette, the shadow levels: each is small
enough to memorize, and constrained enough that two screens of the same
product never look like they were made by different people.

This shows up in how components are composed. A `<kb-card>` doesn't define
its own inner spacing — it uses `--spacing-*`. A `<kb-button>` doesn't
invent its own palette — it resolves `--color-{value}` against the active
brand's tokens. The look changes when the token changes, never when
someone decides, on one specific screen, that "it looks better different
here."

See **Guidelines/Design Tokens** for the full scale in use.
